Installing

  1. Download the ZIP file above.
  2. Right-click the downloaded file, choose Properties, tick Unblock and press OK. This stops Windows warning about the setup program later.
  3. Right-click the file again and choose Extract All.... Open the folder it makes.
  4. Read Effixam Pro User Manual.pdf, chapter 2, "Installing and activating", then double-click EffixamPro-Setup-2026.11.0.exe and follow it.

If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

What you need

  • A computer with Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit), a screen of at least 1366 × 768, and about 300 MB of free disk space.
  • The school's licence file (it ends in .efxl) and the activation PIN, both from Effixam Pro. For a school's own licence, the PIN is given only to the Examination Officer named in the licence, and never sent with the licence file.

What Effixam Pro does

  • No internet needed: everything works on the school's own computer, and nothing is sent over the internet.
  • No worries about power cuts: Effixam Pro saves every entry as you type it, so a power cut loses nothing already typed.
  • Compliance with Ministry and MANEB standards: End of Term exams, with Mid Term exams and tests as continuous assessment, weighted by the Ministry's rule. MANEB JCE grading for Forms 1–2 and MSCE grading for Forms 3–4.
  • Setting up the school: staff, classes and subjects, teachers, and students, one at a time or many at once from the school's Excel list.
  • Entering marks three ways: on the computer, on Excel mark sheets, or from phones over the school's own Wi-Fi or hotspot.
  • Result Sheets for every class and Form, with positions and grades.
  • Filling in the Excel templates that Division, District and Cluster offices send, without copying marks by hand.
  • Report cards for every exam set and every term: remarks from the Form Teacher and the Head Teacher, approved by the Head Teacher, then printed.
  • Safe and private: each person sees only what their role allows, important actions are recorded, and backups are protected with a password.
